Frequently asked questions

Short answers to what people ask most often about Mr. Max.

Who is Max Darkosadze?

A surgeon, an aviation instructor and a lecturer, a writer and a modern philosopher, a sculptor and a filmmaker. He is 31. What holds these far-apart trades together is one thing: precision, and the responsibility that comes with it.

Read more →

Where does the name Mr. Max come from?

Students and colleagues gave it to him — a courteous form of address in Georgian that stuck until it worked like a name. He is known by both: Max Darkosadze and Mr. Max.

What does he lecture on?

Medicine and how decisions get made under pressure, aviation and discipline, ethics and responsibility, and the making of things. The rooms vary: a university, a school, a professional meeting, a conference.

Use the invitation form — who is asking, the date you have in mind, the audience and the subject. Requests are never made public.
