Project

Void Mafia

A cyberpunk game of lies, loyalty and betrayal.

I built this game. The rules of Mafia, set in a cyberpunk world: one side lies, the other side reads — and everything turns on who you decide to believe. It plays in the browser, in Georgian and in English.

Open the game

voidmafia.one — opens in the browser, on a phone or a computer

What is inside

The roles

Mafia, Don, Doctor, Detective, Sheriff, Jester, Phantom and the civilians — each with its own night and its own lie.

Playing together

Lobbies, chat, a voice session and a friends list: a round is built on real conversation, not only on buttons.

Progress

Ranks, seasons, experience, achievements and a leaderboard — every round counts.

Two languages

The whole interface in Georgian and English.

On a phone

It keeps to the home screen like an app, with no store in the way.

Why I recommend it

Mafia is the one game you win not by being quick but by reading people. That is the question my writing keeps returning to: how do you decide when the information is never complete? Void Mafia is the same question, moved to a table.
