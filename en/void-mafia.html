Project

Void Mafia

A game, a social network and tests of the mind, in one place.

Max Darkosadze built this platform. Cyberpunk Mafia rounds; a profile and a feed where anyone can post what they like; and tests of the mind — VOID IQ, the Academy of Formal Logic and playable thought experiments. Results go on a public leaderboard. It all runs in the browser, in Georgian and in English.

Enter

voidmafia.one — opens in the browser, on a phone or a computer

What is inside

The Mafia rounds

Mafia, Don, Doctor, Detective, Sheriff, Jester, Phantom and the civilians — lobbies, chat and a voice session. A round is built on real conversation, not on buttons.

A profile and a feed

The social half: a profile, posts, follows, comments and likes — post what you want, and read what everyone else is posting.

VOID IQ

A test across six kinds of reasoning: pattern recognition, matrix, numerical, logical, spatial and verbal. The result comes back as a percentile — how many of those tested you are ahead of.

The Academy of Formal Logic

Logic, argument, deduction — from syllogisms up. Four levels, a practice mode and a ranked mode, an exam, a handbook, and a daily challenge with a new test every day. Ranks run from student to legend of logic.

Thought experiments, played

The Chinese Room, the brain in a vat and others, rebuilt as scenes you decide your way through. At the end it names the position you actually hold: functionalist, biological naturalist or mysterian.

A public leaderboard

Results stand in the world, in the country and among friends — by week, by month and all time. Seasons, ranks and clans; a test left unfinished does not count.

Two languages, and a phone

The whole interface in Georgian and English; it keeps to the home screen like an app, with no store in the way.

Why he recommends it

Mafia is the one game you win not by being quick but by reading people. The logic academy and the thought experiments are the other side of that question: how we reason, and why we believe what we believe. It is the question his writing keeps returning to — and Void Mafia is where it can be played rather than only read.
