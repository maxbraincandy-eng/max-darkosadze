The Max Darkosadze Foundation

Skill is only worth what it gives back.

The Max Darkosadze Foundation was established in year with a single, unglamorous purpose: to put resources where talent already exists but opportunity does not. It works in the three fields its founder knows best — medicine, aviation and literacy — and it works mostly outside the capital, where the gap between ability and access is widest.

What the foundation does

Its programmes are deliberately small, practical and measurable. The foundation does not fund conferences about problems; it funds the specific thing that removes them.

Principles

  • Fund people, not prestige. Every lari should end up in the hands of someone doing the work.
  • Go where the need is, not where the cameras are.
  • Mentorship is part of every grant. Money without guidance is a gesture, not a programme.
  • Publish the numbers. Anyone who gives has the right to see where it went.

Support the work

The foundation is funded by private donations, by partner clinics and companies, and by the author’s royalties. If you would like to support a scholarship, donate equipment, or offer your own time as a mentor, write to foundation@example.com.

Programmes

  • Scholarships in medicine

    Annual scholarships for number medical students from regions, covering tuition, textbooks and travel to placements.

  • Equipment for regional clinics

    Surgical and diagnostic equipment delivered directly to district hospitals, with training for the staff who will use it.

  • Wings programme

    Subsidised flight training for number cadets a year who could not otherwise afford a licence, with instruction hours donated by volunteer instructors.

  • Books for regional libraries

    Medical, technical and general-interest titles supplied to school and village libraries across region, in Georgian.

  • The debrief seminars

    Free seminars for hospital teams on checklists, briefings and error prevention — the aviation safety culture applied to clinical work.
