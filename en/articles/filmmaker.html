The Filmmaker

Directing, writing and acting — the part of the work with the longest public record

1 min read

Of everything on this site, the film work is the part with the longest public paper trail — it is listed, dated and credited, and anyone can check it.

He is credited as director, writer and actor. In 2016 he directed and starred in the romantic comedy Cowards, made in the United States; his credits also include Star Wars Zero. The full and current filmography is on his IMDb page.

Tbilisi and New York

He was born in Tbilisi on 26 August 1995 and has worked between Georgia and New York since. The two cities show up in the work in different ways: one supplies the material, the other the industry.

The same discipline, a different room

Asked what a film set has in common with an operating theatre or a cockpit, he gives the answer he gives about everything: a crew, a plan, a briefing, and one person who answers for the result. The consequences are lighter. The method is not.

Current credits, dates and titles: IMDb. Behind-the-scenes and new work: Instagram.
