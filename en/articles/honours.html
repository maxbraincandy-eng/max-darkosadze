Honours and Recognition

The distinctions awarded to Max Darkosadze, and what they were given for

date1 min read

He keeps the medals in a drawer, which is either modesty or filing, depending on who is telling the story. The record itself matters, because awards are one of the few public statements a profession makes about what it values.

State and civic honours

  • Name of honour (year) — awarded by body for reason.
  • Name of honour (year) — for reason.
  • Honorary citizen of city (year).

Medicine

  • Award (year) — for contribution to speciality in Georgia.
  • Honorary member of society (year).
  • Award (year) — for the programme introduced at hospital.

Aviation

  • Award (year) — for number hours of accident-free instruction.
  • Instructor of the year, organisation (year).
  • Award (year) — for work in mountain flight training.

Academic and literary

  • Title, university (year).
  • Literary prize or nomination (year) — for book.
  • Award (year) — for popular science writing in Georgian.

What he says about them

An award is a receipt. It tells you that something was delivered. It does not tell you whether anyone is still using it.

Max Darkosadze

Colleagues note that he attends the ceremonies anyway, and always brings students.
