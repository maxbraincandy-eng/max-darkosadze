Max Darkosadze

Known to many simply as Batoni Maksi

A surgeon at the operating table, an instructor in the cockpit, a teacher in the lecture hall, a writer at the desk — and a citizen in the public square.

Few careers touch so many disciplines at once. Max Darkosadze — known across Georgia simply as Batoni Maksi — has spent his working life at the meeting point of precision and responsibility: in surgery, where a single decision changes a life; in aviation, where discipline is the difference between routine and disaster; in teaching, writing and philosophy, where experience is turned into something others can use; and in public work, where it is returned to the community that made it possible.

Each of these fields demands a lifetime. Together they describe one person, and one consistent way of working.

In his own words

The most difficult operation of my career is the one I decided not to perform. I still believe it was right. I have never stopped checking.

The Surgeon’s Hands

Confidence built on procedure survives a bad day. Confidence built on talent only survives a good one.

Wings and Discipline

I can teach you what to do in the situations I have seen. I cannot teach you the ones you will meet. So we practise the thinking, not the answer.

The Lecture Hall

If a sentence needs the reader to admire the writer, it is doing the wrong job. Clarity is the only respect a writer can offer.

The Written Word

The Max Darkosadze Foundation
